SMALL SMALL PROBLEMS 🇰🇪 - HOW TO MAKE YOUR VIDEOS
====================================================

You have everything in this folder:
- studio.html = your cartoon generator (double-click to open in Chrome)
- Episodes/ = 20 ready scripts (Scene + dialogue + CapCut notes)
- Captions/ = 20 TikTok captions + hashtags (copy-paste)

3 STEPS TO CREATE A VIDEO (5 mins each):

1. OPEN STUDIO
   Double-click studio.html -> type topic -> GENERATE -> PLAY
   Screen-record it (Xbox Game Bar: Windows+G, or phone record)

2. EDIT IN CAPCUT (free)
   - New project -> Ratio 9:16 (TikTok)
   - Import your screen recording
   - Auto-captions -> Subtitles ON, Bold font
   - Audio -> Music -> search benga / afrobeats (low volume)
   - Effects -> pop on speech, whoosh on shock
   - First 1 second = shocked face 😱 (stops scroll!)

3. POST ON TIKTOK / REELS / SHORTS
   - Use caption from Captions/ folder
   - Post 7pm EAT daily (Kenya peak)
   - For Part 1/2 trick: end with "Part 2?" and post Part 2 next day
   - Reply to EVERY comment with a funny line
